Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PrakritiDx charges a one-time, non-recurring fee (₹99 for a single report, ₹149 for skin + hair) that unlocks your personalized report and lets you revisit or redo it as often as you like, at no extra cost. This policy explains when a refund applies.

In short: if you paid and never received a working report, you're entitled to a refund. Once your report has been successfully generated and delivered, the purchase is final.

When you're entitled to a refund

  • Your payment was captured, but your report failed to generate due to a technical issue on our end, and the issue wasn't resolved after a reasonable retry.
  • You were charged more than once for the same report due to a payment or system error.
  • You were charged but the plan/category you paid for was never unlocked.

When a refund does not apply

  • Your report was successfully generated and delivered — since this is a personalized digital product created and unlocked instantly for you, sales are final once delivery is complete, the same as most instant-delivery digital goods.
  • You're unsatisfied with the specific content, wording, or recommendations in an AI-generated report that was otherwise delivered correctly. If something in a delivered report genuinely seems wrong or unsafe, please contact us — we take that seriously and will look into it, even though it falls outside typical refund eligibility.
  • Simple change of mind after a report has already been generated.

How to request a refund

Email prakritidx@gmail.com within 7 days of your payment, including the email or session details from your purchase and a brief description of the issue. We'll review and respond within a few business days.

Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method via Razorpay, and typically reflect within 5–7 business days depending on your bank or payment provider.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, or your situation doesn't quite fit the cases above, reach out anyway — we'll look at it on a case-by-case basis.
