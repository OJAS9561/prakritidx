Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PrakritiDx ("we", "us", "our") provides AI-assisted skin and hair analysis blending Ayurvedic principles with modern reasoning. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

  • What you tell us: your name (optional), a description of your skin or hair concern in your own words, and your answers to the guided questions.
  • What you upload (optional): a selfie and/or a lab/test report, if you choose to include them.
  • Your email: only if you use the "Save this report" feature, so we can send you a link back to your report.
  • Technical data: an anonymous session identifier stored in your browser, used to link your intake, payment, and report together. This is not tied to your real identity unless you choose to give us your name or email.
  • Payment data: we never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details. Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay, a licensed payment processor — see their own privacy policy for how they handle payment data.

How we use it

  • To generate your personalized Ayurvedic constitution report, using Google's Gemini AI to analyze what you've shared (including any selfie or lab report you upload).
  • To process your payment and unlock your report.
  • To email you a copy/recovery link to your report, only if you request it.
  • To detect and safely redirect situations that may need real medical attention, before any AI analysis happens.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run PrakritiDx. Each only receives what it needs to do its job:

  • Google (Gemini AI) — processes your intake text, selfie, and lab report to generate your analysis.
  • Razorpay — processes payments securely; we never handle your card/bank details directly.
  • MongoDB Atlas — securely stores your intake and report data.
  • Brevo — sends the "save your report" email, only if you request one.
  • Render — hosts our application infrastructure.

How long we keep it

Your intake answers and generated reports are kept so you can revisit them later (including via the email recovery link) without paying again. If you'd like your data deleted, contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.

Your choices

  • Every field except payment is optional — you can use PrakritiDx while sharing only what you're comfortable with, though a more complete intake gives a more accurate report.
  • You can request access to, or deletion of, your data at any time.
  • Clearing your browser's local storage removes your device's session link to your reports (the email recovery link, if you've used it, will still work).

Health information

Details about your skin, hair, or general health that you share with us are used solely to generate your personal report and are handled with the same care as the rest of your data described above. We do not use this information for any purpose beyond delivering your report.

Children's privacy

PrakritiDx is intended for use by adults (18 years and older). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a minor has used the service and shared personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as PrakritiDx grows. Meaningful changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy, or requests to access/delete your data, can be sent to prakritidx@gmail.com.
